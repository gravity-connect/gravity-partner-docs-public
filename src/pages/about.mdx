# Open by design. Powerful by nature.

## Gravity Connect is the first open standard purpose-built for VPP/DERMS & OEM integrations to enroll and control DERs. 

The Gravity Connect standard is a free, open source specification usable by any distributed energy resource (DER) OEM and any virtual power plant (VPP) vendor. The integration template, which supports many device types, is reusable across different programs; each OEM integration is compatible with all VPP vendors that support the standard.

* **OEMs**

    Build one integration for every program, from enrollment to commands

* **VPPs / DERMS**

    Join our network of device partners with one integration

## The major components of each integration

* **Enrollment**

    Optional modules that support four enrollment flows (and counting!) that allow each program to support the most appropriate user experience, whether devices are installed and managed by the program or by individual customers.

* **Telemetry Data and Reporting**

    Send and receive device data in near-real-time for monitoring device performance.

* **Control**

    Orchestrate devices individually or as groups, with flexible near-real-time group management and specific commands that make the most of each device type.

* **Security**

    Each data transfer is secured with OAuth 2.0 or HMAC authentication. Most enrollment flows share only device-specific identifiers, not PII.

## Partnering with the industry

The Gravity Connect standard was developed in response to existing open standards, which support highly structured integrations that, while customizable, are difficult to reuse for multiple programs. Existing open standards also lack support for DER enrollment; each integration must be supplemented with an enrollment flow using other tools, such as email or securely shared files, rather than near-real-time APIs. 

**We thought the industry deserved better.**

The Gravity Connect standard was [released in 2022](https://virtual-peaker.com/news/virtual-peaker-launches-gravity-connect-api/) through a partnership between Virtual Peaker and a V2G OEM, who saw that the industry lacked a standard that fully supported VPP integrations. Since then, nearly 20 organizations, including DERMS and OEMs, have partnered to revise and upgrade the standard to support new device types, simpler enrollment flows, additional telemetry data, and more.

The specification is maintained by Virtual Peaker in collaboration with utilities and certified partners. Current Gravity Connect partners primarily focus on Grid-Edge DERMS and behind-the-meter assets such as residential batteries and hot water heaters.
