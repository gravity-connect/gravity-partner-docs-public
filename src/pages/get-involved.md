# Get Involved

If you build DERs and you’re ready to join our network of programs, contact us to begin the certification process.

If you would like to add support for a new device type, partner with us to design the specification.

We welcome contributions, amendments, and suggestions.



Contact the Gravity Connect team at [gravity-connect@virtualpeaker.com](mailto:gravity-connect@virtualpeaker.com), and we’ll get back to you within a few days.



Join our mailing list [here]() for release notes and updates from the Gravity Connect team.
